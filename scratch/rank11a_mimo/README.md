# Rank 11a offline MIMO feasibility spike

This spike stops the retained-evidence feasibility test. The repository state
at `61558c2` contains no phase-coherent, independently driven 2x2 acoustic
matrix. It therefore cannot support a measured MIMO gain, a measured rank-2
claim, or a two-stream OTA experiment.

This is an evidence-availability result, not evidence that the MacBook/Pixel
physical channel is rank one.

## Frozen question and decision

[`preregistration.json`](preregistration.json) was written before candidate
outcome analysis. Its primary hypothesis requires a fixed-total-digital-power,
setup-inclusive 1 MiB two-mode gain of at least 1.35x over best-speaker/SIMO in
at least two of three geometries. It also requires:

- two independently driven outputs and two independently retained inputs;
- one common output-column phase reference and continuous receive clock;
- at least five matrices in near-field, centered 3-ft, and lateral 3-ft cells;
- repeat coherence at least 0.95, p90 residual phase standard deviation at
  most 15 degrees, and p90 amplitude coefficient of variation at most 0.15;
- actual mode-2 bitwise GMI sufficient for QPSK rate 1/2 on at least 40% of
  payload-weighted bins; and
- sounding, feedback, activation, update, CP, pilot, control, and gap overhead.

The audit result is `STOP_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`.

## Retained-corpus audit

[`retained-corpus-audit.json`](retained-corpus-audit.json) is generated by
[`audit_retained_corpus.py`](audit_retained_corpus.py) against the frozen source
revision. It scanned 351 tracked files and 30 HIL data/evidence summaries.

| Evidence property | Frozen corpus result |
|---|---:|

| Full phase-coherent 2x2 `H[k]` matrices | 0 |
| Artifact paths referenced but absent from the worktree | 9 |
| Qualified Pixel transmit columns | 1, Mac left speaker |
| Qualified Pixel receive rows | 2 logical direct mappings |
| Required three-geometry, five-repeat cells | 0/3 complete |

The specific failure modes are:

1. The Cyrinx 2 ledger and drive qualification explicitly use only the MacBook
   left speaker. Two Pixel input rows make that a 1x2/SIMO observation, not
   2x2 MIMO.
2. `channel.json` retains scalar SNR/noise-PSD summaries. It does not retain
   complex `H[k]`, a receiver noise covariance, or a second transmit column.
3. The swept-response JSON files retain scalar path magnitude and phase, but
   not a complete port map, common reference, or five repeated matrices.
4. The historical tone-check runner records the left speaker, evaluates a
   gate, and then records the right speaker in another capture. Its own module
   contract says it cannot establish diversity or MIMO. Those columns cannot
   be used for transmit phase or spatial rank.
5. The historical observation that blind L+R duplication increased EVM is a
   useful negative result, but it is not an orthogonal matrix probe and the raw
   capture is not tracked.

The ignored artifact hashes in tracked ledgers preserve result identity, but a
hash cannot reconstruct raw samples or missing channel-column phase.

## Deterministic analysis tool

[`matrix_analysis.py`](matrix_analysis.py) accepts a previously estimated
per-bin complex matrix and enforces the provenance gate before doing matrix
arithmetic. For identifiable inputs it reports:

- receiver-noise-whitened singular values and effective rank;
- best-speaker/SIMO and equal-power MIMO log-det bounds;
- repeat coherence, residual phase stability, and amplitude stability;
- bitwise GMI only when known randomized bits and actual demapper LLRs exist;
- QPSK-rate-1/2 mode-2 bin coverage;
- scheduled ceilings; and
- setup-inclusive 64 KiB and 1 MiB session rates.

The primary MIMO calculation fixes total digital sample power and enforces the
declared one-speaker sample-peak limit on every output. It does not use
water-filling. The input separately records equal measured time-domain sum
sample power and the per-bin symbol power in the normalized `Y=HX+N` domain;
the analyzer does not guess an FFT-normalization conversion. Equal per-speaker
drive at the one-speaker amplitude would add
approximately 3 dB total digital power and is not pooled with the primary
comparison. No output is described as equal acoustic power without calibrated
instrumentation.

The analyzer returns before capacity arithmetic when common phase,
independence, route, clock, noise, or repeat provenance is absent. It also
keeps evidence classes explicit: `synthetic`, `retained_replay`, and
`measured_ota`.

### Allocation-accounting correction

[`analysis-amendment-001.json`](analysis-amendment-001.json) records a
promotion-blocking defect found during independent audit. The first analyzer
required mode-2 GMI support on only 40% of bins but calculated candidate
throughput with an unconditional `streams=2` multiplier over every payload
bin. A partial-rank channel could therefore receive throughput credit for
unsupported second-mode bits.

The corrected analyzer requires an explicit per-mode bin mask and MCS frozen
from calibration repeats before held-out analysis. Its canonical JSON SHA-256
must verify before any candidate rate is computed. For each mode and bin it
credits `bits_per_subcarrier * code_rate` only when held-out bitwise GMI meets
that same threshold. Unsupported allocated bins remain in frame airtime but
contribute zero delivered bits. Mode 2 must be a subset of mode 1 and is
credited only where mode 1 is also usable. The residual recovery scalar is
applied after this masking and cannot restore unsupported bits. Non-synthetic
evidence must source both recovery values from held-out block replay rather
than an assumed scalar.

Deterministic regressions now show:

- 100% mode-1 plus 40% mode-2 support produces a 1.4x scheduled ceiling, not
  the former 2.0x;
- 80% mode-1 plus 40% mode-2 support produces 1.2x and cannot pass the 1.35x
  session-net gate;
- held-out GMI cannot expand the calibration-frozen mask;
- post-freeze mask or MCS changes invalidate its hash; and
- a missing frozen allocation produces no candidate session-rate result.

This correction changes no hypothesis or promotion threshold and examined no
measured 2x2 OTA outcome. The retained result remains
`STOP_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`.

### Synthetic validation result

The seeded full-rank fixture validates formulas and failure gates; it makes no
claim about the room or devices.

| Synthetic metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Minimum held-out repeat coherence | 0.999934 |
| p90 residual phase SD | 0.241 degrees |
| p90 amplitude CV | 0.00505 |
| p10 effective rank | 1.8591 |
| Equal-power MIMO / best-speaker-SIMO log-det ratio | 1.4464x |
| Mode-2 QPSK-r1/2 GMI bin coverage | 100% |
| Fixture 1 MiB scheduled session ratio | 1.99995x |
| Decision | `SYNTHETIC_VALIDATION_ONLY` |

The scheduled ratio is nearly two because this deliberately low-rate fixture
schedules two QPSK-r1/2 streams that both sit below their measured synthetic
GMI. It must not be substituted for the lower 1.4464x log-det ratio, and
neither number predicts the physical bench.

The older `scratch/mimo_vs_mono_throughput.py` is not usable evidence: it draws
unseeded independent Rayleigh matrices, does not use a retained room response,
does not hold total transmit power fixed between one and two streams, omits
session overhead and FEC, and estimates “goodput” by multiplying uncoded symbol
rate by `(1 - symbol error rate)`. Its illustrative multipliers are excluded
from this spike.

## Minimum next acquisition

[`next-acquisition-manifest.json`](next-acquisition-manifest.json) is an exact,
unexecuted design for the hardware agent. Each 8.6907-second capture uses one
uninterrupted stereo Mac render and one uninterrupted two-channel Pixel
capture. It includes:

- two silence intervals for per-bin receiver noise covariance;
- a shared stereo synchronization segment;
- eight two-symbol Walsh-coded matrix estimates on every active bin;
- full-power tx0 and tx1 SIMO control probes;
- independently seeded simultaneous QPSK streams at half the total power per
  speaker; and
- trailing silence that keeps the known macOS end fade out of the probe.

Execution is deliberately staged. Acquire `near-01` and validate route,
channel mapping, sample continuity, orthogonality, clipping, CP support, and
phase recovery before playing the remaining captures. Five valid near-field
captures provide only a near-field feasibility result. The complete Rank 11a
gate requires 15 valid captures: five whole captures in each of optimized
near-field, centered 3-ft, and 18-inch-lateral-offset 3-ft cells. The first two
captures in each cell are calibration; the last three are held out.

Changing the CP, band, power class, geometry, hypothesis, or threshold after
seeing a candidate outcome requires a new preregistration. Correctness defects
found before measured OTA analysis are recorded as immutable analysis
amendments rather than silently editing the frozen preregistration.

## Reproduction

Run only with the repository virtual environment:

```sh
.venv/bin/python scratch/rank11a_mimo/audit_retained_corpus.py \
  --output scratch/rank11a_mimo/retained-corpus-audit.json
.venv/bin/python scratch/rank11a_mimo/make_synthetic_fixture.py \
  scratch/rank11a_mimo/fixtures/full_rank_synthetic.json
.venv/bin/python scratch/rank11a_mimo/matrix_analysis.py \
  scratch/rank11a_mimo/fixtures/full_rank_synthetic.json \
  --output scratch/rank11a_mimo/fixtures/full_rank_synthetic.analysis.json
.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -v \
  -s scratch/rank11a_mimo -p 'test_*.py'
```

In an isolated worktree without its own virtual-environment link, invoke the
same existing Cyrinx `.venv/bin/python` by absolute path. Do not fall back to
the system interpreter.

## Limitations

- The tool begins at an estimated `H[k]`; the PCM-to-matrix estimator is part
  of the next acquisition implementation and is not silently mocked here.
- Log-det is an upper bound under the declared Gaussian noise, power, channel
  knowledge, and stationarity assumptions. It is not delivered goodput.
- The practical scheduled result depends on observed GMI/recovery and exact
  overhead. A rate that exceeds those gates is invalid even when the schedule
  arithmetic itself is correct.
- Three held-out captures per geometry are a roadmap screen, not a broad
  device/room qualification.
